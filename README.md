# Data architecture case studies

Data architecture work from previous roles, written up as anonymised case studies. Each one covers the problem, the design, the decisions and their trade-offs.

| Case study | In short |
|---|---|
| [Marketing data integration, attribution and audience activation](01-marketing-data-integration/) | Unifying web, app, ad-platform, CRM and product data into one warehouse; identity stitching across devices; moving beyond last-click attribution; sending audiences back to the channels. |
| [Data warehouse modernisation](02-data-warehouse-modernisation/) | Replacing manual CSV loads and proprietary ETL with continuous replication, Airflow, a star-schema presentation layer and self-service BI. |

## About confidentiality

This work was done under a non-disclosure agreement. Company and product names, people, internal tools, ticket references and business figures are removed; diagrams are redrawn, and schemas are illustrative. What remains is the design reasoning.
